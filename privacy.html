Privacy Policy — Gishant Publisher

Last updated 20 October 2026

Gishant Publisher is a personal publishing tool. Its only user is its owner, Gishant Singh, who uses it to publish videos he made to his own YouTube channel and other social accounts.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission through Google sign-in, it uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set their title, description, privacy and scheduled publish time, and read the status of those videos. It does not read comments, messages, subscribers' data or any other person's data.

What it stores and shares

It keeps only the IDs and links of the videos it uploads, in the owner's own spreadsheet. It does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of the YouTube API also follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
